Schüco brings smart automation to windows, doors, and sliding elements by connecting them to Matter-based home ecosystems. With the Schüco wireless sensor, open/closed status can be integrated into Homey to support use cases such as ventilation control, heating optimization, and security notifications. Built on Matter over Thread communication, the solution is designed for reliable, energy-efficient connectivity within your Homey setup. In line with Schuco's smart-home approach, the focus is on better comfort, security, and energy efficiency in everyday living.
